# Quick Task 260819-218: Merge Camera and Camera+Description into one scan screen - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-08-19
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Task Boundary

Merge Camera and Camera+Description into one scan screen: keep only Camera in add-food menu, put photo+description as a button to the right of the shutter.

Current state:
- `AddFoodSheet` has two rows: «Камера / Штрихкод» → `/scan` and «Камера + Описание» → `/scan?describe=1`
- `ScanPage` shutter row is torch | shutter | gallery
- `?describe=1` makes the main shutter freeze the frame and show the existing preview + textarea flow instead of sending the photo immediately

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Ряд кнопок на камере
- Four-slot bottom bar: torch | main shutter | photo+description | gallery
- Photo+description sits immediately to the right of the shutter (not at the far right)

### Вид кнопки «фото + описание»
- Smaller than the main shutter (not a second full shutter)
- Pen icon (`PenLine` already used in AddFoodSheet)
- Main shutter stays the primary “just take a photo” gesture

### Claude's Discretion
- Remove «Камера + Описание» from `AddFoodSheet`; keep «Камера / Штрихкод» and the text-only «Описать» row
- After the pen-button capture, keep the existing post-shot screen (preview + `TextareaWithVoice` + Отправить)
- In barcode mode, hide or disable the describe button — it only applies to food photos
- Keep `aifood://add/scan-describe` and the Android «Камера+» widget as aliases that open the unified `/scan` (backward compatible). Do not keep a separate describe-only camera mode as the default path
- Gallery on the scan screen stays; gallery in the add-food sheet stays

</decisions>

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- Main shutter: photo → analyze immediately (today’s `/scan` behavior)
- Pen button: photo → pending preview + description form (today’s `/scan?describe=1` behavior)
- Do not merge the post-shot description form into the live camera viewfinder

</specifics>
